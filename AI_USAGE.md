# Rules on Artificial Intelligence Usage

As you may have seen I used Generative AI for creating a few Markdown
documents, specifically: `docs/FROM_ZERO_TO_PROJECT.md`,
`projectkit/docs/PACKAGE_SH.md`, and `VERIFY_SH.md`.

I have harsh feelings towards Generative AI (e.g., Claude, ChatGPT, etc.), so I
**personally regret** using AI for producing those documents. However, I was
lazy, tired, annoyed at all of my mistakes and losses in progress, and sick of
writting documentation; I did a lot of work without keeping track and did
not want to go through everything and meticulously recollect what occured only
during development (never documented/committed) and what actually needs to be
documented.. again!

In short, I strongly dislike the thought of AI being used to generate code or
documentation, however if I cannot follow my own rules then the rules mean
nothing. I will no longer be using Generative AI to produce documentation *or
code* (AI may still be used if there is a nieche problem that is
"un-googleable" and the response printed is short and **the** correct/only fix,
often requiring further research to validate the *predicted output tokens*
claims).

---

## RULES

1. Absolutely **no** files or directories shall be generated by an AI/agent,
   **empty or not**.

2. AI/agents shall **not** write/generate commit/PR messages, documentation or
   any informational text.

3. AI/agents shall **not** write/generate scripts or any form of *code* meant
   to be executed or parsed specifically via external/internal tools with in
   any manner.

4. Currently the **only allowed** *autonomous agent(s)* are GitHubs runners
   executing allowed Actions workflows; no other AI/agent may make any changes
   or updates to the repository in any manner.
